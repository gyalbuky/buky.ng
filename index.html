About

Olubukola Sharon Adebayo, popularly known as GyalBuky, is a Nigerian dance artist, choreographer, teacher and creative director based in Lagos, Nigeria. Her art is expressed through movement rooted in Nigerian urban dance styles, contemporary movement and femininity. She is the founder of GNGSTR GRLS, a growing female urban dance community. Join her on this journey: work with her, collaborate and experience her art.
